Property Services // Component Maintenance & Upkeep

Proactive building maintenance, water testing and evaluation, California SB 326 balcony repairs, and emergency leak response for portfolio managers and HOA communities.

Proactive Maintenance Philosophy

Preserving Building Envelopes & Reserve Capital

Preventative maintenance stops small seal failures from mutating into six-figure dry rot disasters. Our Property Services division partners with community managers to provide single-source accountability, predictable repair costs, and digital historical archives.
