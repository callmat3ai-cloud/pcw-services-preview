El Nino Prep // Survival Guide

Set up your office and yourself for storm-season surge: authorization limits, tarp rules, and vendor communication.

El Nino Prep

Set Up for a 10++ Fold Surge

  1. Set up your office and yourself for a 10++ fold increase in work orders and requests.
  2. Stock additional common office materials – paper, ink, etc.
  3. Obtain emergency preapproval limits for you HOA - $1,500++
  4. Tarp approval authority
  5. Emergency response authority
  6. Tarps are a two-person job and cannot be installed at night or in unsafe conditions – high wind, slippery roofs, etc.
  7. Multiple bids for leaks do not work in El Nino, pick a trusted vendor and track response and success rates.
El Nino Survival

Surviving El Nino

  1. Recognize, we are the disaster on the National News.
  2. You cannot help everyone at the same time.
  3. Inspections, tarps, estimates, approvals, repairs – all take time.
  4. Let staff know it’s ok to raise your hand when you are overwhelmed.
  5. Schedule periodic staff lunch breaks to decompress.
  6. Make it a social time / potluck / favorite treats.
  7. People expect immediate attention nowadays – that’s not possible.
  8. This will be an extended period of clean up and resolution.
  9. Not all reported, “the ceiling is caving in” are true. We see less than 10 in an entire El Nino.
  10. Not all “it’s leaking in the same place” are true. Up to 65% are in a different location or room.
  11. Demand frequent status communication from vendors.
  12. Remember – we are all on the same team and want to serve our clients.
Vendor Coordination

What a Vendor Needs

  1. Correct Contact information including an email and alternate phone number if available.
  2. Description of the problem – the more information the better.
  3. Let us know if tarping and emergency repairs are approved for each Association.
  4. The pre-approval limit – work with a trusted vendor on this one.
  5. Gate access code.
  6. Any other notes you think might be helpful – speeds up the process.
  7. Provide the information listed above, and we’ll take it from there.
