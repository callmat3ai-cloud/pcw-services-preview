Featured Sourced Guide 01 // Critical Response

El Niño Prep, Survival & Emergency Vendor Guidance

Derived directly from over 37+ years of hands-on contractor experience managing severe storm seasons and atmospheric river surges across Southern California multi-family and commercial properties.

Read Complete El Niño Guide

Core Field Takeaways

  • Pre-Approval Limits: Establish HOA emergency spending limits ($1,500+) before storm clouds gather.
  • Daylight Safety: Emergency roof tarping is strictly a two-person job and cannot be performed in high winds or darkness.
  • Single Trusted Vendor: Avoid competitive bidding mid-surge; align with a proven general contractor.
