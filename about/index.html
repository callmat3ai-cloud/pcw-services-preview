Corporate Heritage

About PCW Contracting Services

Over 37+ years providing exceptional general contracting, structural reconstruction, and envelope maintenance for Southern California community associations and commercial assets.

Established 1989 • Costa Mesa, CA

Trusted Advisors to Southern California Communities

Founded in 1989, PCW Contracting Services has built an enduring reputation across Orange County, Los Angeles County, San Diego, and the Inland Empire as a disciplined general contractor that prioritizes client relationships and structural integrity above all else.

Our guiding philosophy is simple: “Extraordinary Service ~ Exceptional Results ~ Trusted Advisor.” We recognize that construction can feel disruptive, and it is how you feel and remember the communication and professional execution that builds lasting relationships.
