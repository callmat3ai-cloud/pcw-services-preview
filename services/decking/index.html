Project Services Division

Decking & Balcony Systems // Major Structural Reconstruction

Full-scale structural framing rebuilds, cantilever beam repair, and multi-layer waterproof deck coating systems.

Scope of Work & Technical Capabilities

PCW Contracting Services executes this classification under our verified California State License (#699611) with dedicated field supervision, line-item scope accountability, and systematic coordination tailored to occupied properties.

  • Complete structural framing reconstruction of cantilevered balcony decks
  • Galvanized metal lath and polymer-modified cementitious deck coatings
  • Class-A fire-rated pedestrian traffic systems
  • Perimeter edge metal, drip flashing, and door pan sill integration
  • Railing stanchion re-anchoring with waterproof sleeve boots

Who We Serve

HOA condominium communities, apartment complexes, and commercial properties with elevated pedestrian walkways.

Process & Field Communication Approach

We assess the framing, replace damaged joists with pressure-treated lumber, apply robust flashing transitions, and lay durable multilayer deck coating assemblies.

Every project phase is supported by structured homeowner advance notices, on-site project coordination, and project documentation and communication accessible to property managers and board officers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical Technical Inquiries

Water penetrates pinholes or improper perimeter flashings, remaining trapped against framing members and causing dry rot that affects structural framing.

Industry best practices generally recommend a maintenance reseal every 3 to 5 years depending on sun exposure and foot traffic.
